1.3.0

Sea Power 0.9.0
- The mod now needs Sea Power 0.9.0 or newer.
- The CIWS burst sound fix and the gun hit sound fix work with the game's new sound system (sounds delayed by distance).

Gun hit effects
- Every gun shell now gets hit effects by its size: 10 classes by shell mass, also for shells of other mods. Each class has its own effects and size for a hit on a ship, a burst in the air, a splash on the water and a hit on the ground.
- The sizes and the mass limits of the classes are set in the Guns tab. The size applies to the whole effect, also to the spray and mist around a water column.
- Shells in flight get a shape (tiny, small, medium, large) and a colour by the same classes, so big guns look different from small ones. Both can be set per class, or left as in the game. Takes effect when a mission is loaded.

Other
- The "Alternative Mods" menu button also appears when the mod is started by R.A.T.S. Keel (after the start-up load screen is over).
- The funnel smoke group "Nuclear (no smoke)" is now just "Nuclear".
