1.1.0
- Fire smoke: SmokeLifetimeFactor (only the smoke part of the fire animation is slowed, default 10), SmokeFireShare (where the flames end in the animation, default 50%), SmokeDensityFactor (number of emitted fire and smoke particles, default 2), SmokeSortFix (columns of smoke of one ship overlap in the right order). New values reach burning ships at once.
- Neighbouring fires of one ship no longer animate identically (e.g. after a saved game is loaded): each gets its own random seed and start moment.
- Settings window: tabs renamed to Lights and Fires and Smoke, Fire light moved to Lights, Apply keeps the scroll position, the window is a little taller by default.
- The .cfg is no longer polled while the game runs.
